Left Center Right is a multiplayer dice game for three or more players.
The goal is to be the last player who still has chips.

SETUP
Each player starts with three chips.

THE DICE
Each turn uses up to three dice. Each die can show L, R, C, or a dot.

L means pass one chip to the player on the left.
R means pass one chip to the player on the right.
C means place one chip in the center pot. Chips in the center are out of play.
A dot means no action is taken.

TAKING TURNS
A player with three or more chips rolls three dice.
A player with two chips rolls two dice.
A player with one chip rolls one die.
A player with zero chips does not roll, but remains in the game because another
player may pass a chip to them.

WINNING
The winner is the last player who still has at least one chip.
